# Textura do cenário

Arquivo: `italian-fields.png`. Criada com OpenAI ImageGen para este projeto e aplicada ao terreno 3D. É uma imagem gerada, não uma fotografia histórica nem um mapa real da Itália. O modelo, prédios, água e árvores são desenhados em 3D pelo código.

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless albedo texture for a 3D flight game's ground mesh. Create a square 2048x2048 highly realistic true nadir orthographic aerial view of rural northern Italian agricultural ground suitable for a 1944 setting. A continuous mosaic of irregular small fields, mature grass, softly rolling green scrubland, subtle bare earth, detailed plough furrows, sparse hedgerows and tiny scattered shrubs, muted olive and earthy ochre palette, like authentic aerial survey photography. Many small fields visible across a roughly 1.5km by 1.5km area. Natural uneven field boundaries, not a grid. Soft overcast diffuse neutral light, no baked directional shadows. Terrain fills the entire image edge to edge. The image must tile seamlessly especially the top and bottom edges. Absolutely no aircraft, no perspective horizon, no buildings, no vehicles, no rivers, no highways, no text, no labels, no borders, no watermark. Realistic fine photographic soil and foliage detail; avoid cartoon, illustration, polygons, glossy surfaces and excessively saturated colors.
